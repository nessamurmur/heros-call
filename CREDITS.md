# Artwork credits

Hero’s Call 0.1.0 bundles 63 illustrations, each with its own license. Artists
are credited beside the displayed image and in **Art & credits**. The portrait
picker offers 30 character illustrations; other images are chapter scenes.

Original PNG, JPEG and WebP files are bundled unchanged. Framing, scaling,
soft-edge masks and transitions are CSS only. Captions and option mappings are
editorial interpretations, not official Nimble depictions. See [ART-REVIEW.md](ART-REVIEW.md)
for source checks, artist-policy research, selection reasoning and coverage gaps.

## Redistribution and sources

Original module code is MIT. Third-party images retain the licenses above; they
are not covered by the module’s MIT grant. Copyright belongs to the named artists.

Retain each image’s author, source and license notice when redistributing it.
CC BY-SA adaptations keep the applicable share-alike terms. GPL-covered images
remain GPL-2.0-or-later, without warranty; their unchanged PNG source assets are
included directly under `assets/curated/`, with the full GPL text under
`licenses/GPL-2.0.txt`. If distributing modified or converted forms, preserve
the relevant source and license obligations.

Wesnoth’s [copyright policy](https://wiki.wesnoth.org/Wesnoth:Copyrights) identifies
the modifiable assets supplied to its source tree as the preferred source. The
PNG files here are the source-tree originals, not locally generated thumbnails.

Full CC BY 3.0, CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 3.0, CC BY-SA 4.0, CC0 1.0 and GPL 2.0
texts are in `licenses/`. `art-sources.json`, `chapter-art-sources.json` and
`wesnoth-art-notice.json` record sources, attribution, license evidence and hashes.
`asset-checksums.json` covers all bundled assets. The Arcmage, Prismatic and
Ancient Beast collection notices are included there as well. Conquering Corsairs
is credited with its artist as requested in its collection notice.

No collection logos are included. No artist endorsement or approval of this
project’s AI-assisted development is implied. No generative image processing
was used.

## Scope

Native class, ancestry, and item thumbnails are displayed from the user's installed
Nimble system and content packs. They are not bundled or covered by these licenses.
No Originate Genesis or Guild Wars 2 artwork, code, or UI assets are included.
The module's own code is MIT licensed; third-party artwork retains the licenses above.
No artist endorsement is implied. The scope and limits of artist-policy checks
are recorded in ART-REVIEW.md. The artwork was not processed with generative image tools.
