# Artwork review — 0.1.0

Reviewed 2026-10-20. The project accepts Creative Commons and GPL artwork,
including licenses that permit commercial redistribution. No new selection uses
a NonCommercial or NoDerivatives-only license. Patreon support is not assumed
to waive attribution, share-alike, or source-distribution conditions.

## What changed

- **Stormshifter:** Kathrin Polikeit's Elvish Druid, with living foliage and plant
  magic. It conveys the nature aspect; it does not illustrate every beast form.
- **Oathsworn:** Emilien Rotival's Paladin, with a sun emblem, ceremonial mantle,
  and bright armour. The mount and equipment are illustration, not starting gear.
- **Shepherd:** Kathrin Polikeit's Mage of Light, wearing religious vestments and
  carrying a luminous staff.
- **Zephyr:** Amy Clare's Martial Arts, showing unarmed kicks and acrobatic
  movement. This scene is reserved for the calling and its final review, and is
  not offered as a single-character actor portrait.
- Each of the 11 calling defaults and 24 background choices has a distinct
  illustration within its roster. Later training/language chapters may reuse
  relevant motifs; this is not a claim of unique art for every possible journey.
- No ancestry uses the shared woodland fallback.

Character-art gaps remain for **Gnome, Halfling, Bunbun, Changeling, Crystalborn,
Fiendkin, Half-Giant, Stoatling and Turtlefolk**. They show their distinct installed
ancestry symbols, labelled as symbols. Suitable portraits were not established
with sufficient visual fit and attribution in this review. No ordinary rabbit,
stoat or turtle has been relabelled as an adventurer.

The paired options also need dedicated **Shroomling, Minotaur and Construct**
portraits. When the native creator exposes those selected variant names, the
module uses the ancestry symbol instead of continuing to display a tree, satyr
or mud creature as that variant. These fallbacks are not finished character art.

## License evidence

- [Arcmage's repository notice](https://github.com/wtactics/art/blob/master/README.MD)
  covers the `WT/` assets with CC BY-SA 4.0 and identifies individual artists.
  Its exceptions were checked; the newly added WT pieces are Santiago Iborra's.
  The notice is bundled as `licenses/arcmage-readme.txt`.
- [Wesnoth's copyright policy](https://wiki.wesnoth.org/Wesnoth:Copyrights)
  distinguishes GPL-2.0-or-later from CC BY-SA 4.0. Individual image metadata was
  checked instead of treating all portraits as CC. Nine unchanged PNG source
  assets from the upstream 1.16 source tree are included under GPL-2.0-or-later;
  the Jinn is CC BY-SA 4.0. Artist and embedded license evidence are recorded in
  `licenses/wesnoth-art-notice.json`.
  Wesnoth identifies the modifiable form supplied to its source tree as the
  preferred source for these visual assets. These PNGs are included directly,
  alongside GPL text and copyright attribution, rather than only thumbnails.
- [Prismatic Art Collection](https://github.com/hurleyit/prismatic_art)
  licenses its finished artwork under CC BY-SA 3.0. Amy Clare's
  [individual collection page](https://opengameart.org/content/prismatic-art-collection-amy-clare-moody-scenes)
  corroborates the license. The
  [Conquering Corsairs collection page](https://opengameart.org/content/prismatic-art-collection-kaitlyn-peavler-conquering-corsairs-card-art-i)
  requests credit to both Kaitlyn Peavler and the game; both are included.
- [Dragon like character design](https://opengameart.org/content/dragon-like-character-design):
  hcjrArt, CC BY 4.0. The artist's post also links a speed-painting process video.
- [Rat People](https://opengameart.org/content/rat-people):
  nubux, CC BY 3.0. The linked author profile identifies Morgan Strauss.
- [Ancient Beast](https://github.com/FreezingMoon/AncientBeast) separates
  CC BY-SA 4.0 artwork from its AGPL code. Only Scavenger was selected;
  the project's [artist-named source](https://github.com/FreezingMoon/AncientBeast-Website/blob/master/multimedia/artwork/Scavenger_by_Ashirox.jpg)
  establishes AshiRox's attribution. Other candidate creatures with unresolved
  individual authorship were not bundled.

The module's original code remains MIT. The illustrations are separate assets
with their own licenses; they have not been relicensed as MIT. Each distributed
image has a source URL, SHA-256 checksum, author and license record. All image
files remain unchanged. CSS framing does not change the distributed originals.
No generative image processing was used.

## Artist policy checks

The requested check is broader than an AI-training or AI-derivative prohibition:
look for an artist explicitly excluding their work from any project developed
with AI assistance. Such a statement would exclude an image from this project
even if its older open license might otherwise allow redistribution.

No explicit project-wide prohibition was found in the public sources checked
for the artists whose work was added or reassigned in this pass. **This is a
bounded search result, not artist approval, an endorsement, or proof that no
such statement exists elsewhere.** No artist was contacted.

This review covers the new/reassigned selections. It does not claim a fresh,
exhaustive policy audit of every unchanged asset from earlier development iterations.
David Revoy remains excluded in accordance with the owner's expressed preference.
Christina Silliman's Priest Hunter was rejected on visual grounds: its garrote
and assassin theme would repeat the Zephyr mismatch. Several visually appealing
Ancient Beast creatures were rejected for incomplete attribution or poor fit.
NonCommercial/NoDerivatives art and proprietary Overgrowth game assets were not
selected.
